Privacy Policy for Safe Voice

Welcome to Safe Voice. Your privacy and security matter to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Safe Voice collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Safe Voice application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Safe Voice, we may process limited information strictly necessary to provide App features:

  • Google Sign-In Profile Details: When you choose to connect your Google Account, we receive basic Google Sign-In profile details (such as your name, display label, and Google Drive authorization scope for files created by Safe Voice) strictly as authorized by you during sign-in.
  • Voice & Audio Input: Audio and voice recordings captured in the App are processed strictly to deliver the core recording, encryption, playback, and user-requested cloud backup functionality within the App, and are never sold or used for advertising.
  • Basic Diagnostic Logs: Minimal technical and diagnostic logs (such as application version, error states, or general performance metrics) may be processed locally or via platform error reporting to maintain reliability and fix crashes.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected or processed information strictly to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain Safe Voice recording, local encryption, and user-initiated backup features.
  • Authenticate your Google Account securely when you authorize Google Sign-In and Drive backup.
  • Diagnose technical issues, recover interrupted recordings safely, and improve application stability.
  • Comply with applicable legal and platform security obligations.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or voice recordings to third parties or advertisers. Information is only handled or disclosed in the following limited situations:

  • When you explicitly authorize backup uploads to your own connected Google Drive account using minimal app-scoped permissions.
  • With essential platform infrastructure strictly required to run authorized services under confidentiality safeguards.
  • When required by applicable law, valid legal process, or to protect user safety and App integrity.

4. Data Security and Retention

Safe Voice implements strong technical safeguards, including hardware-backed Android Keystore AES-256-GCM encryption for stored audio segments, PIN-protected controls, and encrypted local preferences.

Data is retained only for as long as needed to provide the App's functionality on your device or in your authorized Google Drive destination, or as required by law.

5. User Rights and Choices

You remain in full control of your data and permissions. You can delete recordings directly within the App, disable optional cloud sync at any time, uninstall the App to clear local data, and revoke Safe Voice's access permissions anytime through your Google Account security settings or your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Safe Voice is not intended for children under the age of 13 without appropriate parental or guardian consent, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without authorization.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements, security enhancements, or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Effective Date.
